Our mission

The Assembly's official site is the most reliable source there is. This one just makes it easier to follow.

What legassembly.sk.ca does
  • Organizes everything by official document
  • The complete, legally authoritative source for everything
  • Neutral — never compares or summarizes
What this site tries to do
  • Organizes everything by person: one profile, all their files
  • Translates the jargon into plain language (see the Glossary)
  • Lets you follow what affects you, with no judgment or verdict
