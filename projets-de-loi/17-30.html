Bill 17 — The Saskatchewan Commercial Innovation Incentive (Patent Box) Amendment Act, 2025

Assented to on 2025-05-13 · Sponsor: Warren Kaeding

Amends the Saskatchewan Commercial Innovation Incentive (Patent Box) Act to remove the economic eligibility test and extend a key time limit.

  • Removes the 'economic eligibility test' as a requirement for the tax rebate program
  • Extends a deadline referenced in the Act from 8 years to 10 years
  • Repeals sections 9 and 10 of the original Act
  • Removes a repealed eligibility requirement from the certification renewal process
  • Requires eligible corporations to notify the minister of any change affecting their continued eligibility for the tax rebate
  • Allows the minister to act if there are reasonable grounds to believe a corporation's circumstances have changed its eligibility
  • Most changes are retroactive to June 30, 2017, except the deadline extension which applies from the date of assent

✋ What is “challenging”?

One click on “Ask for an explanation” = a citizen request for the bill's sponsor to explain it in plain language, and for us to keep an eye on it together.

An account (email) is required — one request per person, no anonymous requests. Each bill's total is public: it shows which bills people want explained.
